Hoppy Climb Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

In short: your game progress stays on your phone. We don't run servers and don't collect your personal information ourselves. The game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase; both collect device information as described below. Purchases go through Google Play.

Who we are

Hoppy Climb ("the game") is made by Felik ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. If you have questions, contact us at felikonline@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your device: your best score, gems, puzzle pieces, unlocked characters, purchases you've made, and settings such as sound, music, vibration and language. This information stays on your device. We do not receive it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: ads between runs, and ads you can choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps).

Google uses this information under its own privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy, and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). The "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the ads between runs.

Game statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and make it better, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records game events, such as starting and finishing a run, your score, what ended a run, gems earned and spent, rewarded ads watched, puzzle pieces and characters unlocked, and which screens you open. Along with these, Firebase collects:

  • an app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation of the game) and your device's advertising ID;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) and approximate location (country or region);
  • when and for how long the game is used.

We see this information only as statistics in the Firebase console, not to identify you, and we don't add your name, email or anything you type. Google processes it under the Google Privacy Policy. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the consent you give or refuse for ads also applies here: if you refuse, Firebase only counts events without storing identifiers.

The game also checks with Firebase Remote Config, when it starts and when you come back to it, whether a newer version is out, to tell you about it. That check uses the same app instance ID and sends nothing else about you.

Purchases (Google Play)

In-app purchases (such as Remove Ads and gem packs) are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which items you have bought so they can be delivered and restored. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Linking a Google account (optional)

If you choose to link a Google account in the game's settings, you sign in with Google so your progress can be saved to your Google account and restored on another device. Google shares your basic profile information with the game for this purpose. Your saved progress is stored by Google. You can unlink at any time in the game's settings. If you don't link an account, nothing is sent.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (for ads, game statistics, purchases and the optional account link) and vibration (for game feedback, which you can turn off in settings). It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Progress saved on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data (Settings → Apps → Hoppy Climb → Storage → Clear data).
  • Information collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID at any time.
  • Game statistics collected by Google Analytics for Firebase are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted. Uninstalling the game stops any further collection.
  • For any other request about your information, email felikonline@gmail.com.

Security

Information sent by the Google services the game uses is encrypted in transit.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
